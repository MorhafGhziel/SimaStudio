The Mark

سِمة means a distinguishing mark — the trait that makes something instantly recognisable. It is what every brand we work with already has, and what their digital presence should carry.

03 · The Symbolالرمز06

One sheet.
One fold.
One pin.

Two identical half-rings, rotated 180° and stacked so they touch at a single point. It reads as an S for SIMA without being a typed letter — and the saffron pin marks the fold, like the kasra marks سِمة.
